Sree Padmavathi Venkateswara Foundation (Sree PVF) Essay Contest – 2022 Results

The selection committee was disappointed for lukewarm response by the post graduate and D.N.B students of the across the country. The panel of judges decided none of participant essays were not up to the mark for eligibility of 1st placement or second placement. However, the committee decided to give consolation prize for one essay by Dr. Kapil Akula, DNB PICU Resident, Mehta’s Multispecialty Hospital, Chetpet, Chennai.

The resident’s participation is dishearting for the last two years both by quality and number of participants. Hence with heavy heart the committee decided to discontinue the prestigious unique national essay contest for medical residents.
