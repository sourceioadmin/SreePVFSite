SreePVF is organising the Translation Accelerator Meeting this year at NCBS.

September 1, 2026 devadmin Off Latest Announcement

Translation Accelerator Meeting

Since 2019, SreePVF has been supporting translational research in biomedicine and agriculture, committing close to ₹23 crores across 21 projects throughout India. The science behind these projects is promising, but the path from discovery to translation often faces critical roadblocks — from navigating regulatory pathways and clinical trial timelines to securing early-stage industry alignment and identifying the right pathways to scale and adoption.

To help bridge this gap, SreePVF is organizing the ‘Research Translation Accelerator Meeting’ this year, at the National Centre for Biological Sciences, Bengaluru on September 10-12. Over three days, the meeting will move beyond conventional progress reviews to create direct conversations between our grantees and scientific mentors, regulatory experts, industry leaders, entrepreneurs, technology transfer specialists, and investors.

Together, we hope to identify translational challenges early, explore practical pathways forward, and help promising research move closer to hospitals, farms, and communities, where it can make meaningful impact.

The meeting will bring together invited participants and experts for these focused discussions.
