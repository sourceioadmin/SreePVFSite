Translation Accelerator – Day 2 Plenary

September 9, 2026 devadmin Off News

The plenary session on Day 2 of the Translation Accelerator Meeting, organised by SreePVF at NCBS, brings together leading voices around a central question: how do we turn the potential of research into solutions that stand the test of time?

The session will explore the diverse aspects of translation, from catalysing the journey from research to impact to creating scientific solutions with lasting value.

Bringing these perspectives together underscores that the true value of research is not only in what is discovered, but also in what it enables.

Prof. Vidita Vaidya
